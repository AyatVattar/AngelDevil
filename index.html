YOU WERE TOUCHED

天使の悪魔 — Angel Devil

Despite his angelic appearance, Angel is a Devil whose touch can take away human lifespan. Direct skin-to-skin contact allows him to absorb a person's remaining lifespan. The longer the contact, the more life is taken. The lifespan he absorbs doesn't simply disappear. Angel can store it and transform it into weapons through his halo, with some weapons requiring years of accumulated lifespan to create. Angel himself is reluctant to use this ability. The people whose lives he has taken can haunt his dreams, making the power feel more like a burden than a gift.
